# The writing sample — Baba v. SOS International (ALJ decision, 2017) · WEIGHT: FULL (real, affirmed on appeal)

**What it is.** A 23-page Decision and Order Denying Compensation and Benefits, U.S. Department of Labor, Office of
Administrative Law Judges, San Francisco, case 2015-LDA-00761, issued April 25 2017 under ALJ Richard M. Clark. George
drafted it as judicial extern. A Defense Base Act claim (Longshore Act extended to overseas contractors): an Iraq-born
linguist who worked "outside the wire" claimed PTSD. Decided on the record, no hearing. Six disputed issues. Two
psychologists weighed (Dr. Meisner over Dr. Friedman: objective testing and exaggeration indicators versus subjective
history); findings of fact numbered; claimant found not to have proven a compensable psychological injury by a
preponderance; benefits denied; remaining issues not reached.
**Outcome.** Affirmed by the Benefits Review Board, BRB No. 17-0440, January 30 2018 (public, 6 pages).
**What it proves.** Federal-level legal writing: issue framing, evidence weighing, credibility findings, a clean order. The
kind of document a real judge signed and an appeals board upheld.
**The sentence.** "As judicial extern to ALJ Richard M. Clark I drafted a 23-page Defense Base Act decision, decided on the
record across six issues, that the Benefits Review Board affirmed on appeal (Baba v. SOS International, BRB No. 17-0440)."
