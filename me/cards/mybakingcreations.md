# mybakingcreations.com · WEIGHT: FULL (live business site, his first build, worked on daily)

**What it is.** The website of My Baking Creations, a Bay Area custom cake and cookie bakery (Yana bakes, George builds),
serving since 2012. Static site on GitHub Pages, 44 pages, one 5,000-line CSS file, vanilla JavaScript, all WebP images,
Schema.org on every page, Open Graph, sitemap, six local-SEO landing pages, three blog posts, 700+ products in site search.
Ordering: a guided printed-cookie checkout (Cloudflare Worker + R2 + Queues + server-side PayPal + Resend email), corporate
quote form, order form. A Claude chatbot (Cloudflare Worker, reads the live site) and a Vapi phone helper that answers
Yana's missed calls. Internal Thursday dashboard on Firebase. 25 corporate client logos shown (Google, Meta, Microsoft,
Salesforce, OpenAI, Stripe, Pinterest, PayPal, DocuSign, Gap, ...).
**Numbers (README, April 2026).** About 40 active users a day, 1.1K a month, 95% engagement; Google organic 1,120 clicks per
90 days; ~2,000 monthly visitors on zero ad spend (LinkedIn Featured). Survived and gained during Google's March 2026 spam
and core updates: indexed pages went 44 → 60 in the update window.
**Opened live, 2026-10-27.** Home page: "Family-Owned Since 2012 · Custom Cakes, Cookies & Cake Pops", a 13-slide hero, sections
What We Create (cakes $250–$650+), Latest Creations, Trusted by Leading Companies (10 logos on the strip), Why Choose Us, Request
Your Custom Order. Nav: Order Now, About, Gallery, Corporate Orders, Design Studio, Blog, Contact, Request an Order. 70 images on
the home page.
**For us, not for letters (George, 10/27).** The bakery's PayPal income is what pays the bills; about $70k year to date in 2026. The dashboard's Money tab carries the live figures ($3,990 the week of Oct 21–27).
**Status.** Live. Repo gugosf114/gugosf114.github.io, about 1,400 commits, pushed 2026-10-27. Ops log and Search Console
work recorded in the README.
