# NoiseFile — "Neighbor Noise Complaint Kit" · WEIGHT: FULL (live on Google Play)

**What it is.** An Android app that turns a noise problem into a complaint the city can act on: measure, check the city's
own ordinance, file it the way that city wants. 36 Bay Area cities, 108 city-and-noise-type workflows, every rule read from
the city's current code and quoted verbatim on screen ("THE CODE SAYS · Planning Code 17.120.050(G)(2)"). Local-first: no
account, no analytics, no ads; measurements stay on the phone. One paid unlock, $7.99: sealed PDF report, city form guide,
filled-in city email.
**Status (Play Console, 2026-10-27).** Production, public. Update 0.30.10 published today. Last 28 days: 5 device acquisitions,
2 first opens, 1 monthly active device, $4.99 revenue (one sale). No ratings yet.
**What it proves.** He can read municipal code at scale (36 cities, 52 archived official sources with hashes), turn law into a
product, ship on Play with billing, and keep a strict boundary (a phone reading is never a legal verdict; tests enforce it).
